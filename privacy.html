Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 22, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how our mobile application ("the App") collects, uses, and protects your information when you use our service, connect to hardware via Bluetooth (ESP32), and sign in via Google OAuth or standard account registration.

1. Information We Collect

We collect several types of information to provide and improve our fuel monitoring and ride-tracking services:

A. Personal & Account Information

  • User Profile: Full name, email address, and encrypted password (for standard accounts).
  • Google Account Data: If you sign in using Google OAuth, we receive your basic profile information (name, email address, and profile picture) in accordance with Google's permissions.
  • Vehicle Information: Motorcycle model, engine capacity (cc), and custom fuel prices you input to calculate trip costs.

B. Sensor & Telemetry Data (IoT & Device)

  • Bluetooth (BLE) Data: Real-time fuel level measurements transmitted from your ESP32 hardware module via Bluetooth connection.
  • Location & Speed Data: GPS location, real-time speed, and route logs collected from your mobile device during active rides to calculate mileage, trip efficiency, and fuel consumption.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected data strictly for providing core app features, including:

  • Calculating accurate fuel consumption using ESP32 sensor telemetry combined with your motor model/cc specs.
  • Displaying real-time speed, trip history, route maps, and estimated fuel costs based on your local fuel price input.
  • Authenticating your user account and securing access across your devices.

3. Data Sharing & Disclosure

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal or location data to third parties. Data is shared only under the following circumstances:

  • Google OAuth Services: Authentication credentials handled through Google Sign-In adhere strictly to the Google API Services User Data Policy.
  • Legal Compliance: If required by applicable laws, legal process, or governmental requests.

4. Data Storage & Security

We implement industry-standard security practices to safeguard your personal credentials, location logs, and hardware telemetry against unauthorized access, disclosure, or destruction.

5. Bluetooth & Location Permissions

To operate core features, the App requests permissions for:

  • Bluetooth / Nearby Devices: Required to scan and connect to your ESP32 fuel tracking module.
  • Location Services (GPS): Required to track vehicle speed, map route logs, and discover nearby Bluetooth devices on supported operating systems.

You may revoke these permissions at any time via your smartphone settings, but doing so will prevent real-time fuel and trip tracking.

6. User Data Rights & Deletion

You have the right to access, update, or request the complete deletion of your account and associated ride history. To delete your profile and personal data, contact us at the support email provided below.
